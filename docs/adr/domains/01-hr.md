# ADR-D01: Human Resources & Talent Management Vertical on AgentVerse
Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-07-04 · Source: docs/domains/01-hr/use-cases.md

## 1. Market & Monetization
- **TAM:** Global HR-tech $38B (2024) → $91B (2032). Adjacent proof-points: avg cost-per-hire $4,700 (mostly coordination overhead), 73 min/day/HR-rep on repetitive queries, onboarding touches 54 activities across 11 systems.
- **Buyer persona:** Head of HR / CHRO and TA (Talent Acquisition) leads at 100–5,000-employee firms; economic buyer is the CHRO, champion is an HR Ops manager. Secondary: RPO / IT-staffing founders.
- **Pricing tiers (₹):**
  - **HR Starter — ₹15,000/mo:** ≤100 employees; JD writing+posting, resume screening, policy Q&A; 500 goals/mo.
  - **HR Professional — ₹50,000/mo:** ≤500 employees; + onboarding, offboarding, review cycles, sentiment; 5,000 goals/mo; template library.
  - **HR Enterprise — ₹2,00,000+/mo:** unlimited; full suite + Workday/SAP SF custom integrations; white-label; 99.9% SLA; multi-state compliance configs.
- **Consumption add-on model:** per-unit meters layered on tiers — ₹500/JD posted, ₹50/resume screened, ₹200/interview loop, ₹5,000/employee onboarded, ₹3,000/offboarded, ₹2,000/IDP; ₹50,000 one-time policy-ingestion setup. Overage goals billed above tier cap.
- **Willingness-to-pay:** High for hiring-velocity and compliance-risk reduction; ROI is legible (e.g. ₹53,000/mo saved on payroll queries alone for a 500-person firm).
- **Time-to-first-revenue:** ~2 weeks (policy Q&A + JD drafting ship on Bucket-1 connectors day one).
- **Monetization note:** **Mixed.** The doc-and-messaging UCs (policy Q&A, JD drafting, interview scheduling, freight-free) are fast-cash Bucket-1. But HR's core value (screening, onboarding, payroll, reviews) is **connector-gated on ATS/HRIS** (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, BambooHR, Darwinbox, GreytHR, SAP SuccessFactors) which are **not** in the catalog — these are New-API builds. Land with Bucket-1 Q&A/scheduling, expand once ATS/HRIS connectors ship.

## 3. Connectors Required
- **Existing (catalog · Bucket 1 · no build):** gmail/email, slack, linkedin, github, jira, asana, docusign, google_calendar, google_drive, google_docs, aws (IAM), web_search, document_reader, pdf_generator.
- **New-API (build; medium–high cost):** ATS — Greenhouse, Lever, Workday; HRIS/payroll — BambooHR, Darwinbox, GreytHR, SAP SuccessFactors; ServiceNow; GSuite/O365 admin (account provisioning); Indeed; Outlook; internal LMS; engagement — Lattice, Culture Amp. **This is the gating build cluster** — one ATS + one HRIS connector unlocks 8 of 12 UCs.
- **RPA-portal (Bucket 2 · needs build):** naukri (catalog RPA), benefits enrollment portals (RPA-new, per-carrier — high cost, brittle).

## 4. Knowledge Collections
- `hr-policies` — ingest company leave/travel/expense/CoC/POSH/PF/ESI/maternity policy PDFs & DOCX via document_reader; chunk by section with page anchors for citation.
- `onboarding-playbooks` — internal onboarding runbooks, IT-setup checklists, role-specific 30-60-90 templates.
- `it-setup-guides` — provisioning SOPs per system.
- `company-tone-of-voice` — approved JD samples and employer-brand style guide (for UC-1).
- `org-competency-framework` — role/level skill matrices for L&D gap analysis (UC-9).
- **Ingestion recipe:** crawl the HR shared drive / Confluence space; ingest only customer-owned documents; no copyrighted course catalogs (fetch Udemy/Coursera live via web_search at query time).

## 5. Guardrails & Compliance
- **Regulated:** Yes — EEOC / anti-discrimination hiring law, GDPR + local labour law (India PF/ESI, Form 16), POSH. Payroll and PII data flows are auditable.
- **Mandatory HITL gates:** salary-band exceptions, termination/immediate access revocation, default-plan benefits auto-enroll, contradictory-policy resolution, final review ratings & HR sign-off.
- **Fail-closed policy:** `payroll.*` writes denied by default; `*.delete_access` requires approval (per sample manifest). Blind-screening redaction (name/age/gender) enforced pre-scoring; if redaction fails, block the score.
- **Audit needs:** append-only audit trail for every screening decision, access grant/revoke, and payroll answer — compliance evidence for EEOC and labour audits; RLS-scoped so an employee only ever reads their own records (UC-5).

## 6. Scale Pattern & Cost Drivers
- **Dominant shape:** High-volume-repetitive (screening, queries, JD posting) + Approval-gated orchestration (onboarding, offboarding, reviews).
- **Expected goal volume:** Starter 500/mo, Pro 5,000/mo, Enterprise unlimited — screening is the volume spike (200–500 resumes/role).
- **Cost drivers:** resume PDF parsing + per-resume LLM scoring (token-heavy at 200–500/role); policy-Q&A embedding/retrieval; RPA session minutes for Naukri + benefits portals.
- **Caching/routing levers:** semantic cache on policy Q&A (same questions repeat) and JD templates; route screening to a cheaper/faster model with a verifier pass; batch resume scoring; long-term memory for per-tenant tone and rubric reuse.

## 7. Decision & Phasing
- **Flagship first:** **UC-8 HR Policy Q&A & Compliance Assistant** — pure Bucket-1 (document_reader + slack), instant ROI (400 hrs/yr saved), zero connector build, proves the platform in week one.
- **Fast-follows (Bucket-1):** UC-3 Interview Scheduling (google_calendar), UC-1 JD drafting (LinkedIn/web_search — hold multi-portal posting until Naukri/Indeed land).
- **Connector-gated (New-API build order):** UC-2 Resume Screening + UC-11 Analytics (Greenhouse/Lever first ATS), then UC-5 Payroll Bot (one HRIS), then UC-4/UC-6 Onboarding/Offboarding (HRIS + ServiceNow), UC-7 Reviews, UC-10 Sentiment. UC-12 Benefits deferred (RPA, brittle).

## 8. KPIs
- **Adoption:** % of open reqs using JD/screening agent; policy-Q&A deflection rate (target 85% resolved without HR); onboarding tasks auto-completed vs 54-task baseline.
- **Reliability:** eval pass % on `onboarding-completeness-eval` and screening-rubric eval; blind-screen redaction success 100%; RPA success % for Naukri/benefits (target >90%).
- **Cost/goal:** avg tokens per resume screened; ₹/JD posted; RPA-minutes/enrollment.
- **Revenue/tenant:** MRR by tier + consumption overage; expansion rate from Starter→Pro (unlocked by ATS/HRIS connector adoption); logo retention.
