# Reflexion quality poisoning
## Symptoms
Quarantine, negative usefulness, or recall failures rise.
## Impact
Unsafe or irrelevant lessons could degrade future execution.
## Dashboard queries
Inspect memory safety denials and strategy success deltas.
## Diagnosis
Review provenance, applicability, expiry, feedback, and content digests.
## Mitigation
Quarantine suspect memories and disable cross-session recall.
## Recovery
Delete or expire poisoned records and rebuild verified indexes.
## Verification
Confirm tenant isolation and positive held-out usefulness.
## Escalation
Notify governance for coordinated poisoning or deletion failure.
## Rollback
Pin the prior memory snapshot and use execution-local memory only.
